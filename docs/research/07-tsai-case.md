# 07 — Tsai field case

Evidence: user-provided account in the referenced conversation; no standalone source document was available here.

Key observations: dozens of hard and soft rules were discovered iteratively; apparently obvious subject-distribution conventions were not obvious to the model; changing the master schedule did not automatically update a derived swimming allocation; and a human review period remained necessary.

Phase 0 consequences:

- constraint provenance/category must be explicit;
- unknown preferences must remain unknown rather than becoming defaults;
- the authoritative schedule needs versions;
- derived artifacts must record which version produced them;
- Draft 1 review and local repair are first-class, not signs that generation failed.
